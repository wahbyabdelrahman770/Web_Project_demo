Chicken Alfredo

Creamy pasta with grilled chicken and parmesan. Serves 4.

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast500 g
  • Fettuccine pasta350 g
  • Heavy cream1 cup
  • Parmesan, grated3/4 cup
  • Butter2 tbsp
  • Garlic cloves3

Instructions

  1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper, then grill or pan-sear for 6–7 minutes per side. Rest, then slice.
  2. Boil the pasta in salted water until al dente. Drain and keep a little of the cooking water.
  3. Melt the butter in a large pan and cook the garlic for one minute.
  4. Pour in the cream and simmer for 3 minutes, then stir in the parmesan until smooth.
  5. Toss the pasta in the sauce, loosening with cooking water if needed. Top with the chicken and serve hot.
